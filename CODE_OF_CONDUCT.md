# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Behavior that helps this project:

- being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission

## Financial Data

This project deals with people's household finances. Do not ask a contributor or
issue reporter to share real budget data, account numbers, balances, or an API
token in a public thread. When a bug report needs data, ask for a redacted or
synthetic payload.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/hs737). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect the privacy and
security of the reporter.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will
explain the reason when they do.
