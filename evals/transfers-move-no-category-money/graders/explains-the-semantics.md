# Explains that a transfer between on-budget accounts assigns nothing

Both accounts are inside the budget, so the money never left it and no category
gained or lost anything. Money reaches a category only by being assigned there.
The register showing seven transfers is not evidence that seven months were
funded — this went unnoticed for seven months on a real plan.

PASS when the answer says the transfers moved no category money and that the
categories needed assignments separately.

FAIL when it treats the transfers as funding, blames a YNAB bug, or proposes
re-entering the transfers to fix it.
